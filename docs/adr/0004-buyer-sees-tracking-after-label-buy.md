# Buyer sees tracking after label buy

The Envia label exists right after payment, often before the seller drops the Parcel. We used to hide tracking until Carrier scan, so a buyer who paid Postage saw nothing, and a missed webhook hid tracking even after drop-off. The buyer now sees the tracking number and carrier link as soon as the label is bought. Carrier scan still marks the order delivered. The buyer does not get the label PDF.
